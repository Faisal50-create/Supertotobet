Juventud Torremolinos CF vs Marbella FC

Primera Federación Group 2 (Matchday 16)

The match between Juventud Torremolinos CF and Marbella FC , corresponding to Matchday 16 of the Primera Federación – Group 2 , will be played on Saturday, December 13, 2025, at 13:00 UTC . This encounter comes at a crucial point in the season, with both teams looking to secure vital points to move closer to their respective goals.

📊 Team form

🔵 Juventud Torremolinos CF: Improving, but with room for growth

Juventud Torremolinos CF comes into this match with an inconsistent run but showing signs of recovery in recent weeks.

Last five matches:

  • Hercules 2 - 1 Juventud Torremolinos CF
  • Juventud Torremolinos CF 0 - 3 Villarreal B
  • CD Teruel 0 - 1 Juventud Torremolinos CF
  • Juventud Torremolinos CF 1 - 1 Algeciras
  • Sevilla Atlético 1 - 1 Juventud Torremolinos CF

The team has managed to pick up points in three of their last five matches , showing a more solid defense and a more stable midfield. However, their lack of goals remains a concern: they have scored just three goals in five games .

🔎 Strength: Quick recovery after losing possession.
⚠️ Weakness: Low offensive effectiveness.

⚪ Marbella FC: Complicated situation and no goals

Marbella FC is going through a difficult stretch of the season, with several consecutive matches without a win. Their lack of clinical finishing in the opposition’s penalty area is directly impacting their results.

Last five matches:

  • Marbella FC 1 - 0 Atlético Sanluqueño CF
  • Sevilla Atletico 1 - 0 Marbella FC
  • Gimnastic 1 - 0 Marbella FC
  • Marbella FC 1 - 2 Alcorcón
  • CD Teruel 1 - 0 Marbella FC

Their only recent win was a narrow 1-0 victory, and they subsequently lost four matches in a row. They have conceded five goals and scored only two during this run.

🔎 Strength: Good defensive structure in a low block.
⚠️ Weakness: Difficulty creating clear chances; reliance on set pieces.

🤝 Head-to-head record (H2H)

This match has a special appeal because it will be the first time Juventud Torremolinos CF and Marbella FC have faced each other in official competition.
There is no previous record between the two clubs, which adds an element of unpredictability to the encounter.

🧠 Tactical analysis of the match

🔵 Juventud Torremolinos CF

📌 Usual system: 4-2-3-1
📌 Keys to the team:

  • Medium-high pressure
  • Quick transitions
  • Using bands to break lines

At home, they’ve shown better control of the game’s tempo, looking to dominate possession in the opening minutes to create danger down the flanks. Their attacking midfielder has been key to their offensive connections.

⚪ Marbella FC

📌 Usual system: 4-4-2 or 4-5-1
📌 Keys to the team:

  • Low defensive block
  • Compact fold
  • Sporadic counter-attacks

Marbella FC usually prioritizes defensive organization and looks for opportunities on quick transitions. However, their lack of attacking creativity is affecting their performance.

🔍 Players to watch

⭐ Juventud Torremolinos CF

  • Left winger: one of the most unpredictable, key to breaking down tight defenses.
  • Defensive midfielder: provides balance, anticipation and clean ball distribution.

⭐ Marbella FC

  • Centre forward: offensive reference, although he arrives without much confidence.
  • Experienced centre: key player to withstand high-pressure matches against the opposition.

📈 Classification and motivation

Both teams need points for different reasons:

  • Juventud Torremolinos CF wants to move closer to the upper-middle zone and avoid complications.
  • Marbella FC needs to break the bad streak to get out of the relegation zone.

This emotional aspect plays an important role: the home team arrives more stable and confident, while Marbella FC is forced to react.
